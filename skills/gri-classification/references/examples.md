## 1. Composite good, GRI 3(b): LED bow decoration (NY N362060)

Facts: 48 x 48 inch bow; 3-dimensional iron wire frame covered with two layers of polyester mesh; LED string lights zip-tied around the edge; holiday decoration, indoor and outdoor; lights only enhance the look.

Candidates: 6307 (other made up textile articles, the importer's suggestion), 8306 (statuettes and other ornaments of base metal), 9405 (lighting), 9505 (festive articles).

Reasoning: the good is prima facie classifiable under more than one heading because it is made of different components, so GRI 1 cannot decide and GRI 3 applies. It is a composite good. Under GRI 3(b) the essential character comes from the iron wire frame: it gives the bow its shape and structure; the mesh and lights are attached to it. Lighting is incidental, which rules out 9405. CBP classified it in 8306.29.00.00 as other ornaments of base metal.

Rejected: 6307.90.98 (textile does not give essential character); 9405 (lighting is incidental).

## 2. Retail set, GRI 3(b): DIY canvas painting kit (NY N361728)

Facts: children's kit with six acrylic paint pods, a brush, a small canvas with an outlined image, a small plastic easel and stickers, packed for retail sale, used together to paint the canvas.

Test for a set (EN X to GRI 3(b)): at least two articles prima facie in different headings; put up together for one activity; packed for sale without repacking. All three are met.

Reasoning: the paints and brush are indispensable to the activity of painting and give the set its essential character. The set is classified as colors in sets in 3213.10.00.00.

Rejected: 5907 or 5911 for the canvas, 3926 for the easel, 4911 for stickers (none gives essential character).

## 3. Parts and accessories: magnetic assembly (HQ H341222)

Facts: neodymium ring magnet on a stainless steel yoke; mounted on a rotor shaft; the yoke transmits torque to the shaft; supplied for turbocharger and EGR valve actuators.

Candidates: 8505 (magnets), 7326 (other articles of steel), 8483 (transmission elements and parts), 8503 (parts of motors).

Reasoning: the importer argued composite good under GRI 3 with the magnet giving essential character (8505). CBP applied Section XVI note 2 first: parts that are goods of a specific heading of chapter 84 or 85 go to that heading (note 2(a)). The assembly transmits torque, so it is a transmission element of heading 8483. GRI 3 is never reached because GRI 1 with the section note decides. Affirmed 8483.90.80 (statistical suffix today: 8483.90.80.80).

Lesson: read the section note on parts before arguing essential character.

## 4. Incomplete article, GRI 2(a): sectional sofa components (NY N362411)

Facts: left-facing loveseat, sofa and armless sofa units with wooden frames, foam, polyester upholstery and pop-up recline mechanisms; not sold separately; connected to other units to form a sectional.

Reasoning: heading 9401 covers seats. Each unit, as presented, has the essential character of a seat, so GRI 2(a) brings it into the heading for the complete article even though it is only part of the finished sectional. Chapter 94 note 2 requires floor-standing articles; these are. Upholstered, wooden frame, household: 9401.61.60.11.

Rejected: 9401.41 (convertible seats with integrated folding mattress; these have none); 9401.99 (parts; each unit has the character of a seat, not a part).

## 5. GRI 3(c): climbing quickdraw (NY N361345)

Facts: two aluminum carabiners joined by a textile sling; for climbing; sold singly and in sets of six. The same ruling covers rope clamp ascenders (9506.99.60.80).

Reasoning: the quickdraw is a composite of textile and aluminum. Headings 6307 and 7616 each cover part of it and equally merit consideration. Neither component clearly gives essential character, so GRI 3(b) fails. GRI 3(c) sends it to the heading last in numerical order: 7616, other articles of aluminum, 7616.99.51.90.

## 6. Garment by fiber, GRI 1 then GRI 6: women's cashmere sweater (NY N344142)

Facts: women's knit turtleneck sweater, laboratory result wholly of cashmere, fewer than 9 stitches per 2 cm, extends below the waist.

Reasoning: GRI 1 puts knit sweaters in 6110. GRI 6 then compares subheadings at each level: of wool or fine animal hair (6110.1x), of cashmere (6110.12), wholly of cashmere (6110.12.10), women's (suffix 20): 6110.12.10.20.

Facts that decided it: knit versus woven (chapter 61 vs 62), fiber content by weight, stitch count, gender. If any of these is missing, ask (example 9).

## 7. Footwear by upper material: athletic shoe (NY N361892)

Facts: below-the-ankle athletic shoes; textile upper with 3D printed rubber or plastic overlays that make up the majority of the external surface area of the upper (esau); rubber or plastic outer sole with a foxing-like band; valued over $12 a pair.

Reasoning: chapter 64 headings split on the material of the upper and outer sole (chapter 64 note 4). Because rubber or plastics make up most of the esau, heading 6402 (outer soles and uppers of rubber or plastics) applies, not 6404 (uppers of textile), which the importer suggested. Then GRI 6 on the subheadings: other footwear, athletic, over $12: 6402.99.90.05.

Facts that decided it: which material covers most of the upper's external surface (accessories and reinforcements excluded), outer sole material, foxing band, value per pair.

## 8. Revoked-ruling chain: wafer probe cards

- NY K89734 (2004) classified probe cards in 8536.90.80.
- HQ H011054 (2010) revoked K89734 and moved them to 8536.90.40 as "wafer probers".
- HQ H192481 (2014) revoked H011054: probe cards measure or check electrical properties of ICs on a wafer, so they belong in heading 9030. Section XVI note 1(m) excludes articles of chapter 90 from section XVI, so 8536 cannot apply. Final: 9030.82.00.00.

How to use it: if `cross_search` returns K89734 or H011054, call `ruling_status`. Both are `revoked`; the linked ruling points to the next step. Cite H192481 (in force) and say that the older rulings were revoked.

## 9. Missing facts: ask instead of guessing

Description: "women's sweater, imported from Italy."

The heading (6110) is clear, but the subheading depends on fiber content (cotton, wool, cashmere, man-made), and knit versus woven decides the chapter. Return `abstain: true`, a best guess at 4 or 6 digits in the rationale, and questions in `missing_facts`:
- "What is the fiber content by weight?"
- "Is the fabric knitted or woven?"
- "Is it wholly of cashmere, or a blend?"

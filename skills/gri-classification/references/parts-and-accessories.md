# Parts and accessories

Parts are the most common source of misclassification. Always read the section notes with `get_notes("section", "XVI")` or `get_notes("section", "XVII")` before placing a part.

## Section XVI (chapters 84 and 85), note 2

Unless note 1 of the section, note 1 of chapter 84 or note 1 of chapter 85 excludes them:
- **2(a)** Parts that are goods of any heading of chapter 84 or 85 (other than 8409, 8431, 8448, 8466, 8473, 8487, 8503, 8522, 8529, 8538 and 8548) go to their own heading. A gear, a pump, a motor or a transmission element is classified as that good even when it is a part. Example: a magnetic assembly that transmits torque stays in 8483 (HQ H341222).
- **2(b)** Other parts suitable for use solely or principally with one machine or group of machines go with that machine (or to 8409, 8431, 8448, 8466, 8473, 8503, 8522, 8529 or 8538).
- **2(c)** All other parts go to 8409, 8431, 8448, 8466, 8473, 8503, 8522, 8529 or 8538 as appropriate, or to 8487 or 8548.

Facts to ask for when missing: what machine the part is for, whether it is used solely or principally with it, whether the part is itself a good named by a heading.

## Section XVI, note 1: exclusions

Note 1 removes whole groups from section XVI, for example parts of general use of section XV (note 1(g)), and articles of chapter 90 (note 1(m)). Wafer probe cards measure electrical quantities, so they are chapter 90 goods and cannot be in 8536 (HQ H192481).

## Section XV, note 2: parts of general use

Screws, bolts, nuts, springs, chains and similar goods of base metal are "parts of general use". They are classified in their own headings (7318, 7320, 7315...) and never as parts of the machine they go into.

## Section XVII (vehicles, aircraft, vessels), notes 2 and 3

- Note 2 says "parts" and "parts and accessories" never cover these, even when they are identifiable as vehicle parts: (a) joints and washers, and vulcanized rubber articles of 4016; (b) parts of general use of base metal (section XV note 2) or similar plastic goods; (c) tools of chapter 82; (d) articles of 8306; (e) machines of 8401 to 8479 and their parts (except radiators for these vehicles), articles of 8481 or 8482, and articles of 8483 when they are integral parts of engines or motors; (f) electrical machinery of chapter 85; (g) chapter 90; (h) chapter 91; (ij) arms; (k) lighting of 9405; (l) brushes of 9603.
- Note 3 says a part of chapters 86 to 88 must be suitable for use solely or principally with those vehicles.

## Chapter 90, note 2

Parts and accessories of chapter 90 instruments: goods of a specific heading of chapter 84, 85, 90 or 91 stay in their heading; other parts suitable solely or principally for one instrument go with it; the rest go to 9033.

## Procedure

1. Is the part itself a good named by a heading? If yes, that heading (note 2(a) style rules).
2. Is it excluded by a section note (general-use parts, chapter 90 goods)?
3. Is it solely or principally used with one machine or vehicle? If yes, the parts provision for that machine.
4. Otherwise the residual parts heading.
Record the note number you relied on in `gri_path`.

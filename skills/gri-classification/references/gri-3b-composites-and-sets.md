# GRI 3(b): composite goods and retail sets

Use only after GRI 1 and GRI 2 fail, that is, when the good is prima facie classifiable under two or more headings and no heading or note decides.

## Is it a composite good?

Components that are attached to each other to form a practically inseparable whole, or separable components that are adapted to each other, mutually complementary, and together form a whole that would not normally be sold in separate parts. Example: an LED bow on an iron wire frame (NY N362060).

## Is it a retail set?

All three must hold (Explanatory Note X to GRI 3(b)):
1. At least two different articles that are prima facie classifiable in different headings. Six identical items are not a set.
2. Put up together to meet a particular need or carry out a specific activity.
3. Put up for sale directly to users without repacking.

Example: a painting kit with paints, brush, canvas, easel and stickers (NY N361728).

Not sets: goods packed together with no common activity (a mug and a scarf), or bulk packs for resale.

## Which component gives essential character?

Look at, in no fixed order:
- the role of the component in the use of the article (often decisive),
- bulk and quantity,
- weight,
- value.

Say which factor you used and why. When factors point different ways, CBP rulings often rely on the role in use (see NY N361728, where the paints were "indispensable" to the activity). If you truly cannot pick, go to GRI 3(c): the heading last in numerical order among those that equally merit consideration (NY N361345, quickdraw in 7616).

## Checks before you use GRI 3

- Section and chapter notes first. Section XVI note 2 or chapter 64 note 4 can settle a case that looks like a composite (HQ H341222).
- GRI 3(a) first: the heading with the most specific description wins, except when each heading refers to only part of the materials or of the set, in which case they count as equally specific and you move on to 3(b).
- After GRI 3 picks the heading, apply GRI 6 for the subheading using the material or component that gave essential character.
